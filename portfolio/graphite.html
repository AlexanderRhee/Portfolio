Stable crack propagation in nuclear graphite

OSU · MEENA Group · Research

Specimen preparation and test-fixture development

Objective

I support research on stable crack propagation in nuclear graphite through specimen preparation and the development of bending-test fixtures.

My contribution

My work includes cutting, polishing, and notching graphite specimens, along with CAD design and 3D prototyping of sample-holder and bending fixtures for research conducted in partnership with Kairos Power.

I also perform fractography and load and unload samples in the RAD SEM.

My fixture-design work focuses on combining three support spans in a single jaw to simplify changes between test configurations.

Three support spans in one jaw

Design goal

I redesigned the bending-test jaw to accommodate three span sizes in one component. The aim is to reduce the need to change jaws between configurations and reduce the machining costs associated with separate jaws for each span.

CAD iterations and printed prototypes

I developed successive CAD iterations and produced 3D-printed prototypes between revisions. This provided a physical prototype at each stage of the design process.

Current progress

The completed work includes the CAD iterations and printed prototypes. Further evaluation in the bending setup will establish the fixture’s performance. Reduced jaw changes and machining cost remain design objectives.
