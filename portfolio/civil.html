Civil Engineering Internship

City of San Ramon · Civil Engineering Intern · October 2024–June 2025

Municipal design, fieldwork, and technical review

My contribution

During my internship with the City of San Ramon, I worked on standard detail drawings, GIS measurements, quality-control inspections, residential plan reviews, field surveys, and traffic-safety concerns.

AutoCAD details and GIS measurements

I designed standard detail drawings and checked maps using AutoCAD and GIS. One drawing was a curb detail. I also calculated and verified measurements for internal data and contractor quotes.

Inspections and residential plan reviews

I performed quality-control work for potential commercial and city projects through onsite and online inspections. My technical review work included:

  • Plot plans for City Village, a 404-home residential project.
  • Improvement plans for Iron Horse Village, a 125-home residential project.
  • Improvement plans for Woodside Canyon, a 54-home residential project.

I also participated in field surveys to locate property lines.

Traffic safety and resident concerns

I analyzed traffic-safety concerns, provided recommendations, and drafted responses to residents’ concerns about traffic safety.
